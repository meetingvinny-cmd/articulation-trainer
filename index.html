                Voice and audio

                Tap once, then the app can read to you. iOS will not release the voice list until you do.
                Dictation
                Dictation is the phone's own built in speech to text. When it is on, your spoken audio is sent to Apple or Google to be turned into words, the same as when you dictate a text message. It is the only thing in this app that ever leaves the phone, and it is the only thing that needs a signal. Turn it off and you count your own fillers by ear instead, which trains the ear better anyway.
                Not tested yet. Run this once from the Home Screen icon and once in Safari.
